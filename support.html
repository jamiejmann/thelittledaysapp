Questions

How do I share Little Days with my partner?

Open Family, then make an invite code. It’s six characters. Your partner — or a grandparent or nanny — creates their own account, enters the code, and they’ll see the same baby, live, on their phone.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Family → Settings → Delete my account, and type DELETE to confirm. If you were the only caregiver on a baby, that baby’s logs are deleted too. You can also email us and we’ll help.

Is our family’s data private?

Yes. Only the people you invite can see your baby’s information. We never sell your data, and there are no ads, tracking or analytics. You can read the full privacy policy.

What does Little Days cost?

$9.99, paid once, for the whole family. There are no subscriptions, in-app purchases or ads. The App Store app is coming soon.
