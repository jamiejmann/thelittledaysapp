Made for the whole family

The little things that make long days feel lighter — and let everyone stay in the loop.

👨‍👩‍👧

One baby, every caregiver

Share a 6-character invite code with your partner, a grandparent or a nanny. They sign in with their own account and see everything, live.

🍼

One tap — or log it later

Feed, Diaper or Sleep — that’s it. Time a feed or nap live, or log a past one if nobody started the timer. Pumping and medicine fit right in, and every save has an Undo.

✅

Today’s goals

See today’s feeds and diapers next to typical newborn ranges for the first 8 weeks. Dots fill in as you log. Typical ranges, not medical advice — you can hide the card in Family → Settings.

🕒

Last 24 hours, at a glance

Wet and dirty diapers, feeds and sleep, counted for you — and, if you like, a poop’s color, texture and amount, the details your pediatrician asks about. Days can start at your baby’s birth time.

⏰

A gentle feeding alarm

It counts down to the next feed and chimes when one is due — shared with everyone in the family.

📏

Growth, the gentle way

Weight, length and head, with growth percentiles based on the WHO Child Growth Standards, so you can see how your baby is growing.

📋

Share a report with your pediatrician

In one tap. A clear summary of feeds, diapers, sleep and growth, with the questions you want to ask the doctor. Tap Share to send it from your phone, or print it.

Tap the mic at the top of Today to say a feed or a diaper — speech stays on your phone (needs a supported iPhone or browser). Typical diaper counts by age sit next to today’s totals, and a simple supplement log shows who gave today’s dose and a days-given summary for checkups.

Questions families ask

How do I share Babyway with my partner?

Open Family, then make an invite code. It’s six characters. Your partner — or a grandparent or nanny — creates their own account, enters the code, and they’ll see the same baby, live, on their phone.

How do I delete my account?

In the app, go to Family → Settings → Delete my account, and type DELETE to confirm. If you were the only caregiver on a baby, that baby’s logs are deleted too. You can also email us and we’ll help.

Is our family’s data private?

Yes. Only the people you invite can see your baby’s information. We never sell your data, and the app has no ads, no ad tracking and no analytics. You can read the full privacy policy.

How is Babyway different from other baby apps?

Babyway is free to try for 14 days, then $9.99, paid once. That unlock includes voice logging (speech stays on your phone), a one-page checkup summary with a days-given total for supplements, typical diaper counts by age next to today’s totals, no ads, and one unlock for every caregiver, each with their own login. No subscription. The app has no ads, no ad tracking and no analytics, and we don’t sell or share your baby’s data.

What does Babyway cost?

Babyway is free to download, with 14 days of every feature. After that, one Family Unlock is $9.99, paid once, for voice logging (speech stays on your phone), a checkup summary, typical diaper counts by age, no ads, and every caregiver, for every baby you add (twins and future siblings too). It’s a one-time purchase through Apple. No subscription. Babyway comes to the App Store in early 2027.
